# Superstore-Sales-Performance-Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project is an interactive Excel dashboard built using Superstore sales data from Kaggle. The dashboard analyzes sales performance across time, regions, product categories, customer segments, shipping modes, and top-selling products.

The goal of this project is to demonstrate Excel skills in data cleaning, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI creation, dashboard design, and business insight generation.

## Key Insights
1. Technology generated the highest sales among all product categories.
2. The West region produced the highest sales compared to other regions.
3. The Consumer segment contributed the largest share of sales.
4. Standard Class shipping generated the highest sales among shipping modes.
5. A small group of top products contributed significantly to overall sales.

## Business Recommendations
- Focus marketing and inventory planning around high-performing Technology products.
- Study the West region sales strategy and apply similar approaches to lower-performing regions.
- Create targeted campaigns for Consumer customers while also growing Corporate and Home Office segments.
- Optimize Standard Class delivery experience because it supports the largest share of sales.
- Monitor top-selling products regularly to support better stock planning.
